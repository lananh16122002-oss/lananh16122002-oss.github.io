As lead researcher in a three-member team, I study digital vulnerability within scholarship systems, situating scholarships within the framework of datafied educational welfare.

AI-enhanced scientometrics for education

At the AI and Scientometrics in Education Lab, National Chung Hsing University, I used AI-enhanced scientometric methods to analyse and visualise the landscape of education research, with a focus on how AI and generative AI are transforming the field.

With STEAM for Vietnam I develop learning materials and training on AI for teachers, including the Octo AI and AI for Impact projects, and piloted ‘AI Journey to Dien Bien Phu’, which trained 17,000 educators across 500 schools.
