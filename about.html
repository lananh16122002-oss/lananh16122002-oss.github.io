About

I am a master's student in Research and Innovation in Higher Education (MARIHE), based in Krems. My work looks at how AI and digital systems are reshaping education, from the training teachers receive to the infrastructures that decide who gets a scholarship.

I trained in English Language at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, where I began researching intercultural competence and language teaching. Since then I have worked in academic affairs at PoPoDoo Vietnam Education and developed AI literacy curricula and teacher training with STEAM for Vietnam. In 2026 I joined the AI and Scientometrics in Education Lab at National Chung Hsing University in Taiwan for a research exchange.

I am happy to hear from researchers and practitioners working on AI in education, higher education systems or language teaching.
